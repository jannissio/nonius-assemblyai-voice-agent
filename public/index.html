DESIGN WITH EVIDENCE

What supports this approach

Research motivates the design. Our tests establish only what they measure.

Keep the reporting strategy human

NewsInterview studies journalistic dialogue and model-generated follow-ups. Its simulated interviews reveal limits in topic management and recognizing answered questions. This motivates manual coverage checks; it does not prove that Nonius improves reporting.

Spangher et al., ACL 2025 ↗

Support the interviewer’s judgment

A study with 17 qualitative interviewers explored AI-generated follow-ups through a researcher-mediated setup. Its findings motivate discreet suggestions, control over timing and clear explanations. It did not validate this application or establish improved newsroom outcomes.

Zhang et al., 2025 ↗

Protect the conversation

A related CHIWORK 2026 paper highlights divided attention, rapport, disclosure and accountability. Nonius therefore speaks on request, identifies its involvement and leaves decisions to the reporter.

Zhang et al., CHIWORK 2026 ↗

Keep claims attached to their source

The Society of Professional Journalists calls for accuracy, context and verification using original sources. Every Nonius cue must cite an exact transcript excerpt. That check establishes where the words appear, not whether they were recognized correctly or whether the claim is true.

SPJ Code of Ethics ↗

What this prototype does

  • Streams speech through AssemblyAI.
  • Checks exact transcript evidence for suggested follow-ups.
  • Uses predefined question templates.
  • Keeps original excerpts alongside reporter annotations.

What remains unvalidated

Journalist usefulness, effects on rapport, performance across accents and languages, and behavior in sensitive interviews need real user studies. Synthetic tests cannot establish these outcomes.

Audio and privacy

Live audio goes to AssemblyAI for transcription. Transcript context goes to its LLM Gateway for analysis. The app holds interview audio in your browser and keeps no interview content in server logs. Provider processing has its own terms. Closing the page clears the notebook unless you download it.
