# Proposed practitioner pilot — not yet conducted

## Question

Does Nonius help a journalist obtain useful clarifications without increasing
distraction or degrading rapport, compared with a transcript and manual notes?
The engineering benchmark cannot answer this question.

## Design

Recruit 6–8 practicing reporters for an exploratory usability pilot. This number
is a feasibility choice, not a power calculation or evidence of sufficient power.
Use two comparable fictional source interviews, assign order AB/BA, and alternate
which scenario is paired with Nonius. A trained source follows a script but may
respond naturally. Obtain agreement for recording and cloud processing; provide
a local/manual alternative and an easy withdrawal path. Do not use confidential
reporting material in this first pilot.

Freeze the prototype, scripts, interview outlines and rubric before sessions.
Have two reviewers who did not implement the system independently annotate
opportunities and assess questions with the condition obscured where feasible.
Document any unblinding and disagreements. Investigators must not silently change
the rubric to make results favorable.

## Measures

- Useful clarifications obtained / opportunities defined by the reviewer rubric.
- Irrelevant, redundant, leading or unsupported follow-up suggestions, including
  cases where the answer was already in context.
- Whether the reporter noticed and correctly attributed a source correction.
- Time to locate and check a quotation against audio after the interview.
- Reporter distraction, perceived control, source comfort, and qualitative
  accounts of when a cue arrived too early or too late.
- Latency, provider failures, role-assignment work and whether an interruption
  happened without an explicit reporter request.

Keep raw counts and within-person differences. Report all sessions, exclusions
with reasons, missing observations and adverse experiences. Do not turn a small
convenience pilot into a generalizable accuracy, safety, or commercial claim.
An adequately powered later study requires a meaningful effect size, variance
estimate, recruitment plan and prespecified analysis.

## Decision rule

Proceed to a larger study only if participants can complete the essential tasks
and no unresolved attribution or unintended-speech failure remains. Choose any
quantitative product thresholds with practitioners before viewing the pilot data;
no defensible threshold can be derived from the current synthetic fixtures.

## Why these measures

NewsInterview motivates attention to answered-question recognition and topic
management ([Spangher et al., ACL 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1580/)).
Qualitative interviewing studies motivate control, timing and rapport
([Zhang et al., 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.12709);
[CHIWORK 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30980)). Source/audio review follows the
[SPJ professional standard](https://www.spj.org/spj-code-of-ethics/), not an existing
experimental demonstration of this interface.
